Frequently Asked Questions

What are open when letters?

Open when letters are pre-written messages for someone to open in specific situations — like when they're sad, happy, bored, or celebrating. They're a thoughtful, personal gift that keeps giving over time.

Is openwhen free to use?

Yes! You can create and share open when letter collections for free. Some premium features may be available for power users.

How does the recipient open their letters?

You share a unique link with your recipient. They click the link and see all the letter envelopes. They can open each one when the moment is right.

Can I add photos, audio, or video?

Absolutely! You can enrich each letter with photos, audio recordings, video clips, stickers, and links to make it feel truly personal.

Can I edit a letter after sharing?

Yes, you can edit letters at any time after sharing. The recipient will see the updated version when they open the letter.

How many letters can I create?

There's no strict limit on the number of letters you can include in a collection. Create as many as you like!

Is my content private?

Your letter collections are only accessible via a private link. We don't publicly list or share your collections.

Can I use openwhen for any occasion?

Definitely! People use openwhen for birthdays, long-distance relationships, graduations, deployments, anniversaries, and more.

Do I need an account to create letters?

You can get started quickly without a full account. Creating an account lets you manage and edit your collections over time.
