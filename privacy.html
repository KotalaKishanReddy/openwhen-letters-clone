Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use openwhen, we may collect information you provide directly, such as the content of your open when letters, your email address if you create an account, and any media (photos, audio, video) you attach to your letters.

We also collect basic usage data (page views, feature interactions) to improve the product. We do not sell your data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To store and deliver your letter collections to recipients via shared links
  • To send account-related emails (password reset, important updates)
  • To improve and debug the openwhen platform
  • To detect and prevent abuse or spam

3. Data Storage & Security

Your letter content is stored securely. We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is strictly limited to operations necessary to run the service.

4. Sharing Your Letters

Letter collections are accessible via a private link you control. We do not publicly index or share your collections. Anyone with the link can view the collection, so share thoughtfully.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for session management and authentication. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You can also edit or delete individual letters at any time from your dashboard.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of openwhen after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at our contact page.
